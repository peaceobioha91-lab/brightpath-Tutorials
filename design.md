# design.md: BrightPath style guide

> Paste or reference this in every build prompt so all pages look like one family.

## Feeling
Trustworthy, warm, hopeful. A serious exam centre a parent feels safe trusting, but friendly to students. Think neat exercise book and printed exam timetable, not a flashy startup.

## Colours
| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Deep Purple | `#3B2A78` | Headings, header, footer, main text on light |
| Sky Blue | `#5BC0EB` | Register buttons, highlights (use `#241752` text on it) |
| Chalk | `#FFFFFF` | Main background |
| Lilac Paper | `#F3F1FA` | Alternate section backgrounds |
| Marker Green | `#1E8E5A` | Ticks, "open" indicators, success |
| Slate | `#46425A` | Body text and small text |
| Line | `#D8D3EC` | Thin borders |

Rules: body text is Slate or Deep Purple on white. Never put Sky Blue text on white. Text on Sky Blue buttons is dark purple `#241752`. Check contrast at webaim.org/resources/contrastchecker.

## Fonts (Google Fonts)
- **Headings:** Bricolage Grotesque, bold
- **Body:** Nunito Sans, regular and semibold
- Fallbacks: system-ui, sans-serif

## Type sizes (mobile first)
H1 32px, H2 26px, H3 20px, body 17px, small 14px. Line height 1.5 for body. Keep lines under about 70 characters.

## Shape and spacing
- Corners: 10px on cards and buttons, 999px on small tags
- Spacing scale: 8, 16, 24, 40, 64
- Shadows: almost none. Use a thin 1px border (`#D8D3EC`) instead.
- Vary the layout: do not make every section the same grid of identical cards.

## Buttons
- **Main (Register):** Sky Blue background, dark purple (`#241752`) bold text, 48px tall minimum
- **Second:** white background, Deep Purple border and text
- Plain verbs: "Register", "Chat with us", "Get directions". No arrows needed.

## Signature elements (the memorable part)
1. **Timetable as a printed exam timetable:** a clean grid with day columns and ticks in Marker Green.
2. **"Ask us anytime" note** near the top with a small green dot, telling parents the chat answers day or night.
Keep everything else quiet. Spend boldness only here.

## Images
AI-generated, one consistent style (e.g. warm, natural light, realistic illustration or photo-style), same colour mood as the palette. Nigerian students and classrooms. Add alt text. Do not present them as real students or real results.

## Motion
Almost none. One gentle fade-in on the hero is enough. Respect "reduce motion".

## Phone first
Design at 360px wide first. Tap targets at least 44px. Sticky Register button on mobile is welcome. Keep images small so pages load on slow data.

## Tone of writing
Simple words, active voice, sentence case. Speak to the parent: "Your child's exam, our classes." No big promises, no hype, never promise results.

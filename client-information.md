## 2. Mock Client Interview Findings

The following information represents findings from the simulated client
interview conducted for this capstone project. It does not represent a
real interview with an actual business owner.

-   BrightPath Tutorials provides examination-preparation lessons for
    students.
-   Its target audience includes secondary-school students, prospective
    university students and parents or guardians.
-   The centre receives enquiries from prospective students and parents.
-   The administrator, Joy, handles administrative enquiries and
    registration-related communication.
-   Delayed responses to enquiries can affect how quickly prospective
    students receive information.
-   The business requires a more organised way to communicate its
    services and receive registration enquiries.

## 3. Identified Business Challenges

Based on the simulated interview, the following challenges were
identified:

1.  Delayed responses to enquiries from parents and prospective
    students.
2.  Difficulty providing consistent information about available
    programmes.
3.  A registration process that relies heavily on manual communication.
4.  Limited accessibility to important business information outside
    normal working hours.

## 4. Proposed Digital Solution

Develop a website for BrightPath Tutorials to improve information
accessibility, communication and registration.

The proposed website will include:

-   Homepage introducing BrightPath Tutorials.
-   About page describing the tutorial centre.
-   Programmes page displaying available examination-preparation
    programmes.
-   Timetable and fee information.
-   Online registration and enquiry form.
-   WhatsApp contact integration.
-   AI-powered chatbot for answering frequently asked questions and
    guiding prospective students.

The chatbot will provide general information about the centre,
programmes, fees, timetable and registration process. Enquiries
requiring human attention will be directed to the administrator.

## 9. Proposed Student Registration Process

1.  A prospective student visits the BrightPath Tutorials website.
2.  The student views available programmes, fees and class timetable.
3.  The student completes the online registration or enquiry form.
4.  The submitted information becomes available to the administrator.
5.  The administrator reviews the submission and contacts the
    prospective student.
6.  Registration is confirmed after the necessary arrangements are
    completed.
